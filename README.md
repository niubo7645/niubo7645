## About Me

I am Bo Niu, a graduate researcher interested in the application of artificial intelligence to computational biology and drug discovery.

My current research focuses on protein-protein interaction (PPI) binding affinity prediction, structure-aware protein representation learning, and computational protein design.

My previous research experience includes polymer composites, self-healing epoxy coatings, surface engineering, and advanced functional materials.

## Research Interests

- Protein-Protein Interaction (PPI) Affinity Prediction
- Protein Representation Learning
- AI-Driven Protein Design
- Computational Drug Discovery
- Deep Learning for Molecular Sciences
